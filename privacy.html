Privacy policy

Your prices.
Your perspective.

Priceglade processes your personal price book in protected local app storage.

Effective October 25, 2026 · Priceglade 1.0 · Publisher: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir

Local records

Products, brands, stores, categories, purchase dates, quantities, currencies, amounts, notes, chosen source excerpts, scan text and preferences are processed locally. Priceglade has no developer account, backend, tracking, advertising or analytics integration that receives these records.

Camera, selected images and text recognition

Camera permission is requested when you choose Camera. Apple’s photo picker provides only the images you select; the app does not request broad Photos read access. You may also choose an image from Files. Apple Vision recognizes text on the device. Images are temporary, are not uploaded by Priceglade and are not saved in its price book or full backup. Keep your original separately.

Recognized text can be wrong. A local text draft may be retained, including recognized lines and edits. No purchase is saved automatically: you must review the fields and choose Save. Recoverable draft Trash also retains text.

Apple purchases

Apple manages the one-time Lifetime Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. Priceglade reads verified StoreKit entitlements on the device. Apple credentials and payment details are not entered into or sent to the developer through the app. Apple’s App Store and purchase practices apply separately.

Files, exports and backups

You choose whether and where to save or share. Full JSON backups include all retained records, text drafts, notes and Trash. CSV exports contain confirmed purchases and may include notes and chosen source excerpts only when you enable those options; raw scan text and drafts are excluded. Inspect exported files and protect your copies.

Your selected Files providers, receiving apps and operating-system/device backups use their own settings and policies. Priceglade does not implement developer cloud sync or automatically upload records or diagnostics.

Retention and recovery

Archives and recoverable Trash retain content. The app has no permanent-delete control. Imports create fresh archived copies and preserve current work and preferences. Deleting the app removes its local app storage; save a full backup outside that storage first if you need your records. Exported copies and device backups remain managed separately by you and the relevant providers.

If saved metadata cannot be read, the app offers a way to preserve the unreadable original before an explicit fresh start. The developer does not automatically receive that file.

Support

If you email support, we receive what you choose to send and use it to address your request. Priceglade does not automatically attach price records, receipt images, backups or diagnostics. Send only the minimum useful information; do not send passwords or Apple credentials.

This website

This static website contains no advertising or analytics script. GitHub Pages hosts it; the hosting provider’s privacy statement applies to website requests separately from local price-book records.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@arvinify.com. We will update this policy and applicable App Store responses when practices change.
