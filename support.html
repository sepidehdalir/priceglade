Guide & support

Know the price
behind the pack.

Start with purchases you actually made. No account is required.

Version 1.0 is in production. It has not been submitted, approved or released. Screens use original fictional examples.

Record your first purchase

In Book, choose Add purchase. Select or create a product, enter its store, category, date and currency, then the quantity per pack, number of identical packs and total paid for all those packs. Optional brand, notes and a source excerpt stay with your record. Choose Review purchase, check every field and the normalized unit price, enable the confirmation switch and Save.

For three 500 g packs costing USD6 in total, enter 500 g, 3 packs and USD6 total. The equivalent quantity is 1.5 kg and the unit price is USD4 per kg. Enter the total paid, not the individual pack price. A recorded zero price is valid; a missing price is not. Future actual-purchase dates cannot be saved.

Compare two pack offers

Open Compare. Enter both pack quantities, units, identical pack counts and total prices in the same currency. Choose Compare equal quantities. This does not save a purchase. Use the separate actual-purchase action when you want to record one.

Metric mass, avoirdupois ounces/pounds, metric liquid volume, explicitly labeled US or Imperial liquid units, and items are supported. Mass, liquid volume and item counts cannot be interchanged. There is no density or currency conversion.

Understand your personal history

Open a product in Book. Select a currency when its purchases use more than one. Latest, lowest, median and highest figures use your comparable, non-trashed purchase records. The median is the unweighted median of purchase-entry unit prices; even sample counts average the middle two. The chart has a text history alternative. Sample count, dates and store ties provide context. These are not current market prices or a prediction.

You choose whether products, brands and pack types belong together. Search current names, aliases and saved purchase facts. Renaming a library item retains the historical labels saved with each purchase. Insights groups only recorded spending, separately by currency; it is not a complete household budget.

Correct, Undo, archive and recover

Open a purchase to correct its fields, review and save. Undo in Book restores the previous successful in-session change. Archive retains a product’s history. Move a product or purchase to recoverable Trash to exclude it from normal comparisons and totals. Settings > Trash & recovery restores it; restored products start archived so the free active-product limit never blocks recovery. No permanent-delete control is provided.

Recognize text with Lifetime Pro

Choose Scan item text, then Camera, an owned photo or an image in Files. Recognized lines remain unconfirmed. Tap a line to use it for a field, correct every field and explicitly review before saving a purchase. Save a text draft for later to resume after relaunch. Discarded drafts remain recoverable. Images are used transiently and are never retained with the book or backups; keep the original separately.

English (U.S.) and French recognition are available. Supported images are bounded to 20 MiB, 40 megapixels and a maximum 20000-pixel dimension; processing uses a smaller oriented image. A cancelled or failed scan saves no purchase. Existing text drafts remain readable and may be finished within free product limits if Pro access changes.

Save a full backup

In Settings choose Save full price book backup, then a Files destination you control. The versioned JSON includes all retained records, scan text, archived items and Trash. Images are not included because they are not retained. Protect your copy and keep it outside the app’s storage before deleting the app or changing devices.

Choose Import backup as archived copies, select the saved JSON, review the counts and choose Import fresh archived copies. Current work and preferences stay in place; imported products receive fresh IDs and start archived. Repeating an import creates another set of copies. Invalid or oversized backups are rejected without replacing current work.

Export confirmed purchases as CSV

With Pro, open Settings > CSV export. Choose optional notes, source excerpts and any filters, then Save CSV in Files. Raw OCR text and drafts are excluded. CSV is for using confirmed records elsewhere; use the free full JSON backup for recovery.

Lifetime Pro and Restore Purchases

Free includes ten active products and the core manual workflow, comparisons, personal statistics, corrections, archives, recoverable Trash and full backups. Lifetime Pro adds more active products within device limits, new scans, advanced filters and CSV. It is a one-time non-consumable for Priceglade, with no subscription. Apple displays your localized price before purchase.

Use Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen with the Apple account that purchased Pro. Pending, cancelled, failed or unverified purchases do not grant access. Existing records remain accessible if access changes. Apple’s standard license agreement applies.

Device storage limits

Both plans have resource bounds: 10000 products, 100000 purchases, 10000 stores/brands/categories combined, and 1000 text drafts with at most 20000 characters each. The library and full JSON backup budget is 20 MiB. Archived and trashed content continues to count. Keep regular backups in a location you control.

Contact support

Email hello@arvinify.com with the app version, device/iOS version, steps and visible error. Priceglade attaches no records automatically. Do not send passwords or Apple credentials; share only the minimum appropriate information.
