The price behind
the pack.

Keep the package size and total you actually paid. Priceglade puts compatible quantities on the same footing.

01

Record an actual purchase.

Enter the product, store, date, currency, quantity per pack, number of identical packs and total paid for all packs. Review every field before saving.

02

Compare equal quantities.

Compare two unsaved pack offers per kilogram, litre or item. Metric, US and Imperial units are explicitly labeled. Currencies stay separate.

03

Build your perspective.

See your recorded latest, lowest, median and highest unit prices, date range and sample count. Your own history gives the figures context.

Read the text.
Keep your judgment.

Lifetime Pro recognizes text from Camera, an owned photo or an image in Files, using Vision on your device.

Tap recognized lines to fill fields, correct the draft and review the complete purchase before Save. Text recognition can be wrong. Images are temporary and are not retained in the price book or backups; keep your original separately.

Saved text drafts remain available after relaunch and if Pro access changes.
